# Growth Audit - Hairstyle Try On

**Live:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/ai-hairstyle-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/ai-hairstyle-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic you already have, ready for the Christmas party season. Each one is shown next to your screen today and rebuilt as a working screen in a clickable prototype of your flow. Your design, unchanged - only the sequence moves.

| # | Idea | Expected impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Show their haircut before the paywall | Paywall CR +20-30% |
| 02 | Sell the one-time decision too | Payers +15-25%, fewer trial cancels |
| 03 | Fix the camera path | Try-on completion +20%+ |
| 04 | Ask for permissions after value | Onboarding completion +5-10% |
| 05 | An exit offer with a real offer | Offer CR +10-15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps - not measured lift for Hairstyle Try On. Prices come from the app's own in-app screens and its App Store listing (Premium Year 34,99 € and 19,99 €, Premium Week 8,99 €, 30 Coins 3,99 €); photos are the app's own onboarding imagery. `screens/` holds the current flow as captured.
