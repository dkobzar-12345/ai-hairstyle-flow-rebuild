What this is

We opened AI Hairstyle the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at yours: installed it, went through onboarding and the first try-on, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas - not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, shown next to the screen it changes and built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours - your portrait tiles, gold gradient buttons, gold check rows, the trial toggle, the Save 92% plan cards and your gift offer card. Only the sequence changes.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us - we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
